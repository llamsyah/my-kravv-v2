## Purpose and product philosophy

MY KRAVV is a private, personal investment research and reasoning workspace. It helps the owner capture observations, investigate companies, develop and challenge reasoning, record conclusions, and inspect how those conclusions evolve. The owner retains judgment and decision ownership. The product is not an automatic trading assistant, price-prediction service, market-data terminal, or generic AI chatbot.

The useful outcome is recoverable work and an understandable record of reasoning, including uncertainty. Templates assist thinking; they do not certify quality. AI supplies questions and critique; it does not establish truth or silently become the author. Flexible entry paths matter: an informal observation need not become formal analysis, and formal writing need not wait for AI approval.

**Flexible before commit; deliberate and historically consistent after commit.** Formal historical tracking centers on deliberately committed Thoughts, not every temporary note, inbox transfer or autosave. Recovery/authorization remain essential, but do not require a permanent pre-commit lineage product, event sourcing or a history of every edit. MY KRAVV is primarily for the owner's own use; hypothetical public audiences, surveys or productization do not justify extra MVP complexity.

Distinct content types protect this flexibility. A fast capture, a personal notebook entry, an editable analysis, an intentionally staged candidate, and a committed Thought version have different meanings. Saving one must not silently create another. See the [workflow contract](02-WORKFLOWS-AND-INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md) and [conceptual domain](04-DOMAIN-AND-PROVENANCE.md).

## Deliberate exclusions

Initial MVP excludes custom template management, a standalone general AI chat product, automated trading, predictions, market feeds, source scraping, internal document hosting, collaboration, unrelated notification systems, extensive preferences, and incidental global Kalender/Explore destinations. An image icon or toolbar is not authority for attachments, uploads, export, comments, or a new workflow.

Existing V2 Refine is historical technical evidence. The new AI Review priorities do not require carrying Refine into primary V3 Company navigation. No V2 roadmap, data model, visual style or earlier provider authorization is inherited as a V3 implementation decision.

No archive/trash system is required for Penampungan, Explore notes, writing drafts, Reference records or committed Thoughts. Company-level destructive policy is a separate open concern below; this exclusion does not remove existing V2 company functionality.

## Success criteria for owner review

The proposed MVP succeeds when the owner can save and resume private work, distinguish informal material from committed conclusions, deliberately record and compare versions, use external references without storing source files, and request bounded AI critique without losing ownership or control of cost. Manual workflows remain usable when AI is unavailable. Acceptance should be supported by the [validation and recovery cases](07-VALIDATION-AND-RECOVERY.md), not by visual resemblance alone.

## Settled owner decisions

**P1–P8 are resolved by the owner and supersede the first draft's open alternatives.** Their implementation representations remain subject to [T1–T7](06-TECHNICAL-ARCHITECTURE.md#open-decisions--technical-questions); technical evaluation must honor these contracts rather than reopen them.

| ID                                 | Settled product contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P1 — Temporary capture             | Company is optional during Quick Capture. Explicitly move a capture to Explore or Ruang Tulis, requesting a destination company if absent. Remove the inbox entry only after the destination is successfully saved; on failure keep the capture, and deduplicate ordinary retries. Unneeded captures may be permanently deleted with clear confirmation. No archive/trash or permanent transfer lineage is required.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| P2 — Live candidates               | **Kirim ke Pemikiran** stages a draft once as an active Working Tree candidate reflecting its latest saved content. Editing stays in Ruang Tulis with no repeated resubmission or committed versions from autosave. **Batalkan Pengajuan** removes only candidate membership; the draft can be staged again. Tinjau & Rekam shows exact latest content; concurrent changes require revalidation/reconfirmation. Successful commit ends pending membership.                                                                                                                                                    |
| P3 — Deliberate identity           | At recording choose **Thought Baru** or **Versi Lanjutan**, selecting the existing Thought for the latter. A company may contain multiple Thoughts, each with multiple committed versions. Never infer identity from similarity, company, headings or AI. A short commit rationale is optional; no lengthy mandatory administration. Recorded contents are exact and immutable; reading an older version does not make it latest.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| P4 — Explicit deletion             | With appropriate confirmation, the owner may permanently delete captures, Explore notes, drafts, Reference metadata and whole committed Thoughts. Whole-Thought deletion includes all versions and Context Snapshots; individual versions cannot be edited or separately deleted. Correct conclusions through a deliberate new version. Draft deletion cannot delete a committed Thought; affected candidates must be safely removed. Reference deletion never alters external documents or committed text. No archive/trash system is required.                                                              |
| P5 — One writing model             | Terpandu, Pendamping and Kosong change assistance around one document/editor model. Switching never deletes, reorders, rewrites or converts text, creates a version, or requires a new draft. Existing inserted headings remain. The six areas are optional guidance; Kosong has none active, Pendamping offers optional side guidance. No three persistence systems, template conversion or custom template manager.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| P6 — Bounded AI discussion         | Clarify/Challenge/Gap Check support manual follow-ups within the same review context. Every request is explicit and accounted. The active draft is primary; the owner may select Explore/Reference material, with understandable outgoing context. Personal text incorporated into that draft is included, not magically excluded. No hidden extra notes/company data/Context Snapshot, URL fetching or automatic rewriting/commit. Identify reviewed snapshots, visibly stale results after draft changes, and explicit continuation/refresh; keep context/retention bounded. Review never gates recording.  |
| P7 — Minimal references            | Only **Title** and **URL** are required. Source type, publisher, dates/period, tags, description and personal notes are optional. **Gunakan di Ruang Tulis** inserts title and URL, plus only specifically selected personal notes. Keep the original catalog record for reuse. No required taxonomy, uploads, hosted documents, scraping, link verification or automatic AI ingestion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| P8 — Recorded personal perspective | Context Snapshot is wholly optional inside Tinjau & Rekam. Confidence choices are **Rendah, Sedang, Tinggi, Not provided**; explanation/reflection is optional. Every field and the whole section may be skipped. Once committed, the snapshot is immutable with that version: no retrospective edit or separate correction system. Later perspectives belong to deliberate later commits; whole-Thought deletion removes snapshots. Subjective self-report is not objective truth, a quality score, diagnosis or emotional-validity gate. Uncertainty is valid and no psychological pressure is appropriate. |

P4/P8 do not require automatic commits to revise metadata. Committed content and its recorded personal context stay consistent until an explicitly confirmed whole-Thought deletion; noncommitted materials need only sufficient technical state for safe saves, moves, authorization and retry recovery.

## Narrow open owner policy — Company management

**C1 — Company archive/deletion policy:** should destructive Company actions be omitted initially, restricted while related content exists, or permitted with an explicit owner-approved child-record/deletion policy? The choice affects committed Thoughts, notes, drafts, references and candidate cleanup. V2's archive and cascade deletion are evidence, not V3 approval. Do not expose a V3 destructive Company action until this policy is approved; creating, listing, finding, editing and switching companies can proceed independently under separately approved scope.
